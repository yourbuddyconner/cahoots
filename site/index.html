Open the app How you join ↓

Five minutes, in person.

  1. Meet. Bring your circle together, with phones or cards.
  2. Exchange. Stack the cards or scan the codes on the screens; the devices make the keys.
  3. Compare. Read your six words aloud. Everyone's must match.

The full six steps →

What your circle can do.

Chat

A group chat only your circle can read. The keys come from the meeting and never touch a server.

Log in

Your circle becomes an SSH authorized_keys file. Log in to servers and sign git commits with a press of both buttons.

Seal

Encrypt files and messages to one member or the whole circle. age, HPKE and TLS 1.3 take the circle's shared secret as a pre-shared key.

Sign

Sign approvals with a key that never leaves the card's chip. Anyone can check the signature against the chip maker's certificate.

Your messages sit in public. Only your circle can read them.

Cahoots posts to a public feed, like a numbers station. Anyone can see it. Only your circle can read it, post to it, or notice if a post goes missing or moves.

Why carry a card.

      
Your keys live in a chip that can prove they were made there and never gives them up. Every approval is a press of two buttons on something in your hand, so no app, laptop or agent can act for you, and no website can trick you into it. Lose your phone and you lose nothing: tap the card and your chats come back. Stack cards with people you trust and a circle exists in minutes, with nothing sent anywhere.

      
It shows your punk, your contact card, and the six words you read aloud when a circle is made. The hardware and the firmware are open; build your own, or carry ours.
