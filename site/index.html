A small beginning

One meeting.
A lasting connection.

Cahoots calls your private group a circle. You create it together, with the devices you bring.

  1. 01

    Get together.

    Open Cahoots on your phones or laptops. Scan each other’s codes to exchange the material your devices need to create shared keys.

  2. 02

    Check the words.

    Each device shows six words. Read them aloud and make sure everyone sees the same ones. That confirms you share the same secret.

  3. 03

    Take your circle with you.

    Use those keys to chat after you leave. The service delivering your messages never receives the keys to read them.

Trust, with the details attached

Your keys.
Your responsibility.

Cahoots moves the starting point for trust into a meeting between people.

Your devices create a shared secret in person. Later, they use it to protect messages sent over a public relay. Running that relay doesn’t give someone access to your conversations.

The devices still matter. A compromised device can expose its circle, and browser storage has limits. Cahoots is an experimental project; read the threat model before relying on it for sensitive conversations.
