## Rev C: the card and the pile

Why: the badge model was rebuilt as the 3 mm card that stacks (magnets, pads, spring fingers, coin cell, no port). The video showed two badges side by side on pins, which the hardware no longer does.

What changed: the badge model and rig only. `timeline.ts` keeps every frame, so the audit, the hits and `master.wav` stand; the part labels read clear card, e-paper, Baochip-1x, magnets and pads, coin cell. The touch chapter is restaged: the second card descends from above (ease-in-out, then ζ 0.6 for the last 0.75 units with the tilt flattening on the same spring), the pulse runs along the pads at the near edge, the top card lifts off beside the pile on the camera push (`touch.push` drives both), and settles back during `net.recede`. The explode and pair rigs moved in for the thinner card.

Lab notes:

- Whole frame grey, clear card white. The renderer went to an opaque canvas for the new clear plastic, and three.js converts the clear colour for the bound target at clear time, which left an sRGB-encoded value in the composer's linear buffer (corner pixel 90/255, expected 10). Fix: the page colour and the glow are a plane behind the subject, as on the site; the CSS glow is switched off so it is not doubled. Corner pixel now 8/255 before the vignette.
- f680 to f705: the pulse was invisible. A transparent mesh never shows through the clear plastic, because transmission samples only opaque objects. The pulse is now opaque, fades through its colour, and sits at the card's near edge with half of it in the open.
- f641: the closer pair rig cut the top of the pile. Rig eased back and its look-at lowered.
- f745: the lifted card's far edge touched the frame top. Lift lowered from 1.15 to 0.95 units and the screens rig's look-at raised.
- Chrome asks for `/favicon.ico` on every run; the 404 in the render log is that and nothing else.
